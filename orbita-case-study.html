SaaS · Multi-tenant · Social Commerce

The operations system,
now a product.

Órbita OS is a multi-tenant operations platform for social commerce businesses — the kind that sell through WhatsApp, Instagram and TikTok Live rather than a traditional storefront. It runs the full sales lifecycle, inventory, payments and dispatch in one mobile-first system, built for teams with no technical background.

It's for founders running that kind of business today on spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads — and outgrowing both. One deployment, isolated per tenant, so any number of businesses can run on it without touching each other's data.

What's inside

Six modules, one system.

Dashboard
Today's sales, pending balances and stock alerts in one view.
Sales
Full order lifecycle — draft to dispatched — never a dense spreadsheet row.
Inventory
Stock is never hand-edited — it's the sum of every logged movement.
Payments
What's owed is calculated live, never stored as a number that can go stale.
Dispatch
One address, one button, one tap — marking delivered closes the sale.
Multi-tenant core
One deployment, isolated per business — no tenant sees another's data.
Origin

Proved by hand, then productised.

Karigab tested this exact operational logic manually, in WhatsApp and Google Sheets, with a real business and real orders. It worked — and that was the proof. Órbita OS took that same logic and turned it into a platform any social commerce business can run, not just Karigab.
