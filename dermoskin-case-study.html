DermoSkin is the operating system I built for a dermatology clinic — patient records with contraindication alerts, scheduling, informed-consent workflows, financial control and WhatsApp-based follow-ups, all in one system.

It's for a regulated, appointment-based practice running on scattered notebooks and spreadsheets — where a missed contraindication or an unsigned consent form is a real risk, not a paperwork inconvenience.

What's inside

Six modules, one system.

Patients
Full clinical record per patient, with contraindication alerts surfaced before treatment.
Agenda
Scheduling by service and availability, with no double-booking of the same room or provider.
Informed consent
Signature flow per procedure, so nothing is treated without a signed record on file.
Financial control
Income, adjustments and cash position, always tied back to a specific patient and visit.
Follow-ups
WhatsApp-based reminders for the next session, replacing manual phone calls.
Settings
Services, supplies and staff — configured once, referenced everywhere else.
